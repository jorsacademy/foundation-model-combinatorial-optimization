# Model Card

## Intended use

Research and education on multi-task representation learning for small binary combinatorial optimization problems.

## Out-of-scope use

- production planning without independent validation;
- safety-critical decisions;
- claims of optimality;
- arbitrary user-supplied industrial MILPs;
- commercial use under the repository license.

## Training data

Synthetic knapsack, maximum-weight independent-set, and set-cover instances generated by deterministic documented procedures. Exact labels are produced by SciPy/HiGHS.

## Transfer data

Synthetic set-packing instances. The research protocol uses disjoint training-shot, validation, and test seed ranges.

## Known limitations

- fixed supported task registry;
- binary variables only;
- small dense instances;
- one exact label per possibly multi-optimal problem;
- family-specific repair;
- no uncertainty calibration;
- no optimality certificate for neural outputs;
- synthetic data may not reflect operational structure.

## Responsible reporting

Report raw feasibility, repaired feasibility, exact objective gap, and exact-oracle comparisons. Do not report bit accuracy alone as optimization performance.
